6 The 3 Rs of Computer Vision

Once pixels are collected, the AI uses three processes to find meaning:

Recognition

Identifying what is in the image. Is that a person, a car, or a sign?

Reconstruction

Figuring out where objects are in 3D space. How far away is that taxi?

Reorganization

Understanding how parts fit together. These pixels all belong to the same person.
