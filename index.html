CHAPTERS 5–7

The 3 Rs: Stages of Vision

Use the slider to step through the four stages of computer vision: numerical grid → edges → features → reconstruction. See how machines extract meaning from raw numbers step by step.

⏱️ 8–10 minutes
Open Demo
Key Insight: When you look at a photograph, you see meaning instantly. When a machine looks at the same photograph, it sees 36.6 million numbers. Teaching a machine to "see" is teaching it to extract meaning from numbers. That's the entire challenge of computer vision.

How to Use These Demos

  • In the Classroom: Open demos full-screen on projector. Students follow along in flipbook.
  • Online: Share demo links via course site. Students click to open in their browser.
  • Offline: Download the repository as ZIP. All demos work without internet (images embedded).
  • Mobile: All demos work on phones and tablets (responsive design).

Repository Structure

  • demos/ — All interactive demonstrations (HTML files)
  • demos/images/ — Images used in demos (statue, street scenes)
  • flipbook/ — "The Anatomy of Seeing" (13-chapter Computer Vision guide)
  • docs/ — Instructor guide, timing, scripts
  • assets/ — Design system and AI disclosure templates
  • README.md — Full documentation and resources
