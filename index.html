Welcome to Unit 4. This repository contains all interactive demos for learning how machines see. Each demo answers a specific question about computer vision. Click on any demo below to explore, or download the flipbook for full context.

Key Insight: When you look at a photograph, you see meaning instantly. When a machine looks at the same photograph, it sees 36.6 million numbers. Teaching a machine to "see" is teaching it to extract meaning from numbers. That's the entire challenge of computer vision.
