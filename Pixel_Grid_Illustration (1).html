🎯 Key Takeaway: A computer doesn't "see" a city street the way you do. It sees millions of numbers arranged in a grid. Computer vision is the science of teaching machines to find patterns in those numbers — edges, shapes, objects — and turn them into understanding. The jump from 0 → 255 is where it all begins.
